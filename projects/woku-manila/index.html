the kitchen

Nothing hides behind a sauce. Every plate says exactly what it is.

WOKU is built around a single binchotan robata and a chef's counter for eight. Fish comes in daily from Navotas and Toyosu-standard suppliers; rice is aged short-grain, cooked to order. The menu changes with what's good that morning — this is a kitchen that plans around the market, not the other way around.
